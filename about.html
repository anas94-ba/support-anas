Who I Am

I am Anas Baba, a computer engineer who graduated from the Islamic University of Gaza in 2025.

My experience

I volunteered as a teaching assistant in my department for two semesters. I attended labs and lectures and explained them to students, followed up with them, guided their projects, and prepared and graded assignments and exams. I volunteered because of the events in the Gaza Strip since 7 October 2023 and the destruction of its education and health infrastructure. After I proved myself, the university hired me for one more semester, then the work stopped because the university ran short of funds.

In memory of my younger brother

I lost my younger brother in the war, and he was martyred. He was lost in an area that is very hard to reach, so we have not been able to find his remains. May God have mercy on him.

My situation now

I am currently unemployed, and there are no computer engineering jobs in Gaza. There is no internet, and electricity is paid for and costs about $80 a month. My father is elderly and cannot work, and we live on donations. We lost our home at the start of the war and now live in a tent, far from our house because reaching it is dangerous and not allowed.

How your support helps

  • First, covering housing, food and water.
  • Second, buying modern tools. My laptop is an old Core i3 and my phone is a slow J4 whose battery drains fast.
  • Third, paying for electricity and internet so I can work and learn.

My goals

I have prepared myself with projects in data analysis, full stack development and AI agent development, and I am improving my English. If conditions improve, I want to complete a master's degree in artificial intelligence to help people in healthcare.
